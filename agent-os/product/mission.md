# Product Mission

## Pitch
OpenHuman is a keyboard-first email client that helps professionals and power users achieve Inbox Zero faster by providing a blazingly fast, local-first experience with intelligent workflows, keyboard shortcuts, and provider-agnostic email management.

## Users

### Primary Customers
- **Knowledge Workers**: Professionals who process 50+ emails daily and need efficiency
- **Power Users**: Tech-savvy individuals who prefer keyboard navigation over mouse clicks
- **Privacy-Conscious Users**: People who want local-first email storage and control over their data
- **Multi-Account Users**: Users managing multiple email accounts across different providers

### User Personas

**Sarah, Product Manager** (28-42)
- **Role:** Product Manager at a mid-size tech company
- **Context:** Receives 100+ emails daily across work and personal accounts
- **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed inbox, difficulty prioritizing important messages, too much time spent on email management, slow web interfaces
- **Goals:** Reach Inbox Zero daily, respond to critical messages within hours, minimize time in email to focus on strategic work

**Alex, Software Developer** (25-40)
- **Role:** Senior Developer at a startup
- **Context:** Manages multiple email accounts (work, open source, personal), prefers keyboard-driven workflows
- **Pain Points:** Slow email clients, lack of keyboard shortcuts, vendor lock-in with Gmail/Outlook, privacy concerns with cloud-first solutions
- **Goals:** Lightning-fast email processing, complete keyboard control, local data ownership, seamless multi-account management

## The Problem

### Email Overload is Killing Productivity
Most professionals spend 2-3 hours daily managing email, yet 70% struggle to maintain Inbox Zero. Traditional email clients are slow, mouse-dependent, and lack intelligent workflows to help users process messages efficiently. Cloud-first solutions create vendor lock-in and privacy concerns, while existing desktop clients feel dated and lack modern productivity features.

**Our Solution:** A Superhuman-inspired desktop client that combines blazing speed (local SQLite + virtual scrolling), keyboard-first design, and intelligent workflows (Split Inbox, Snooze, Send Later, snippets) to help users process email 2x faster while maintaining full control over their data.

## Differentiators

### Local-First Architecture
Unlike Gmail, Outlook, and even Superhuman, we store all email locally in SQLite. This results in instant search, offline access, zero privacy concerns, and complete data ownership.

### Provider-Agnostic Design
Unlike vendor-specific clients, we work with any email provider via POP3/SMTP. This results in freedom from vendor lock-in and the ability to manage all accounts in one unified interface.

### Keyboard-First Speed
Unlike traditional clients (Apple Mail, Thunderbird), every action is optimized for keyboard shortcuts with a command palette. This results in 50% faster email processing and reduced repetitive strain from mouse usage.

### Intelligent Workflows Built-In
Unlike basic clients, we include Split Inbox, Snooze, Send Later, and snippets out of the box. This results in structured email management that guides users toward Inbox Zero systematically.

## Key Features

### Core Features
- **Lightning-Fast Virtualized List:** Process thousands of emails with buttery-smooth scrolling and instant loading
- **Unified Multi-Account Inbox:** Manage all your email accounts (Gmail, Outlook, custom domains) in one interface
- **Offline-First with Local Storage:** All emails stored locally in SQLite for instant access and complete privacy
- **Conversation Threading:** Automatically group related messages for easier context and faster responses
- **Powerful Search:** Find any email instantly with full-text search across all accounts

### Productivity Features
- **Keyboard Shortcuts Everywhere:** Navigate, archive, delete, star, and manage email without touching your mouse
- **Command Palette (Cmd+K):** Access any action or email instantly with fuzzy search
- **Split Inbox:** Automatically separate important messages from newsletters and notifications
- **Snooze & Remind Me:** Defer emails to exactly when you need to see them again
- **Send Later:** Schedule emails to send at the perfect time
- **Snippets & Templates:** Create reusable responses for common emails

### Advanced Features
- **Read Receipts:** Optional tracking to know when recipients open your emails
- **AI Assistance:** Compose replies, summarize threads, and search semantically (optional)
- **Smart Unsubscribe:** Clean up newsletters and spam with one-click bulk actions
- **Attachments Management:** Easy drag-and-drop with preview and quick access
